The game uses your camera. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

How to play

  1. Stand back so your arms are inside the picture.
  2. 7 spider webs appear around you.
  3. A fly lands in one web. Wave your hand over that web to catch it.
  4. Too slow, and the spider drops down and takes the fly.
  5. 30 flies: 3 seconds each, then 2, then 1.
  6. Catch more than the spider and you win.

YOU WIN!
